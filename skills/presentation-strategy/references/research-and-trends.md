# Research basis and 2026 trends

Sources reviewed 2026-10-16. The workflow recommendations are a practical synthesis. They are not a benchmark showing that this skill improves persuasion, comprehension, or business outcomes.

Consult this reference for requested research or contemporary art direction. Refresh unstable claims when offering current recommendations. Preserve publication dates and evidence classes; a forecast for 2026 may have been published in 2025. An undated page or copyright year does not establish the study date.

## 2026 creative directions

### Canva: Design Trends 2026

[Primary report](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/design-trends-2026/) and [trend collection](https://www.canva.com/design-trends/).

- **Evidence class:** vendor forecast, informed by platform search activity, a Designer Advisory Board, and a global survey of 1,000 creators. The article does not display a publication date or disclose full sampling and weighting details in the reviewed text.
- **Directions:** tactile and analog textures, personal imperfection, calm editorial simplicity, cinematic storytelling, playful digital aesthetics, and regional expression. These coexist rather than defining one universal look.
- **Appropriate use:** choose a relevant visual direction after establishing audience, brand, and purpose. A creative pitch may benefit from expressive collage; an executive decision deck may benefit from restrained editorial structure.
- **Limit:** creator preferences and platform searches do not establish audience comprehension or commercial effectiveness. Do not interpret "imperfect" as permission for factual errors, illegible slides, or careless layout.

### Adobe: Creative Trends 2026

[Adobe's application and methodology article](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/01/08/how-creators-leveraging-adobe-2026-creative-trends), published 2026-01-08.

- **Evidence class:** vendor creative forecast. Adobe describes reviewing campaigns and projects, community feedback, and search patterns, rather than a controlled presentation study.
- **Directions:** sensory imagery, human connection, playful surrealism, and local cultural specificity.
- **Appropriate use:** relevant human stories and regional context can inform imagery and examples; sensory or surreal art can serve a brand or creative concept when suitable.
- **Limit:** commercial visual trends do not justify decorative effects in every deck. Local relevance requires credible context rather than stereotyped motifs. No quantified presentation-effectiveness sample is disclosed in the reviewed article.

### Canva: Presentation Paradox

[Primary study summary](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/ai-presentation-study/). Publication and field dates are not displayed in the reviewed text; do not describe its statistics as a verified 2026 measurement.

- **Evidence class:** vendor-commissioned, self-reported survey conducted with Pure Spectrum, covering 4,000 professionals who regularly build and deliver presentations in eight named countries.
- **Finding:** 71% said they trusted AI to build slides but not to present or tell the story. The article also describes AI use for coaching, drafting, and anticipating questions.
- **Implication:** preparing a presentation can include narrative, questions, and rehearsal support in addition to constructing a file.
- **Limit:** the sample represents surveyed regular presenters, not every worker or audience. Self-reported career and productivity associations are not causal findings.

## Evidence about substance and communication

### BetterUp Labs and Stanford Social Media Lab: workslop

[Primary summary and methodology](https://www.betterup.com/workslop), based on an online survey of 1,150 full-time US desk workers in September 2025.

- **Evidence class:** self-reported workplace survey, not a controlled evaluation of slide generators.
- **Finding:** 40% reported receiving workslop in the previous month. The authors describe AI-generated material that appears polished but lacks useful substance, including slick slides and other workplace outputs.
- **Implication:** review reasoning, context, and recipient effort, not appearance alone.
- **Limit:** this is neither the percentage of AI decks that are poor nor a global 2026 prevalence measure. Avoid extrapolated cost claims without explaining their assumptions.

### Penn State: assertion-evidence slide research

[Research overview](https://www.writing.engr.psu.edu/research.html) and [2011 study abstract and design](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/assertion-evidence-slides-appear-to-lead-to-better-comprehension--2/).

- **Evidence class:** presentation-learning research in technical and educational contexts. The 2011 experiment compared two groups of 55 and 56 participants hearing the same recorded presentation with different slides.
- **Finding:** assertion-evidence slides produced higher scores on complex comprehension and retention questions, with some differences statistically significant. The research overview also describes subsequent work.
- **Implication:** a clear message supported by explanatory visual evidence is a useful option for technical findings.
- **Limit:** do not promise a universal improvement, assign an invented percentage uplift, or require takeaway headlines on every setup or process slide. The design combines several differences and does not isolate every visual ingredient.

### Kobak and colleagues: vocabulary shifts with LLM-assisted writing

[Paper and publication details](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07016), published in Science Advances on 2025-07-02.

- **Evidence class:** corpus analysis of more than 15 million biomedical abstracts from 2010 through 2024.
- **Finding:** abrupt changes in the frequency of some style words were associated with the emergence of LLM-assisted writing.
- **Implication:** generic or repetitive vocabulary deserves editorial attention, alongside meaning and specificity.
- **Limit:** aggregate vocabulary patterns are not reliable proof of authorship or poor quality in an individual slide. Do not present punctuation or a single word as a scientific AI detector.

## Durable practitioner and operational guidance

- [Duarte: Slidedocs visual documents](https://www.duarte.com/resources/guides-tools/slidedocs-visual-documents/), undated in the reviewed text: distinguishes independently read visual documents from projected slides. Useful format guidance, not controlled proof of meeting outcomes.
- [Y Combinator: A Guide to Demo Day Presentations](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/), 2016-07-25: practitioner guidance for a specific investor setting. It does not define the structure of every presentation.
- [Microsoft: accessible PowerPoint presentations](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accessibility/powerpoint/make-your-powerpoint-presentations-accessible-to-people-with-disabilities), maintained guidance without a displayed publication date in the reviewed text: reading order, alt text, contrast, unique titles, captions, and other accessibility practices. Automated checkers have limits and do not replace inspection or relevant assistive-technology testing.

## Applying the sources

Separate four kinds of statement when researching or recommending a style:

1. What a dated forecast predicts.
2. What a platform or survey observes, with population and method.
3. What research tested in a defined setting.
4. What the presenter or skill author recommends as a synthesis.

Avoid unsupported rules about an eight-second attention span, one universal slide count, a fixed words-per-slide formula, or a fashionable aesthetic guaranteed to persuade. Choose a form because it serves the audience and evidence, then verify the actual result.
