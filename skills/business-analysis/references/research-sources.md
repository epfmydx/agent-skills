# Research basis and refresh boundaries

Reviewed 2026-10-14. The skill is an original, practical synthesis for software delivery. Its prompts and examples are recommendations, not an official BA standard or evidence of measured effectiveness.

| Primary source | Supported insight | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [IIBA: 2026 Global State of Business Analysis public findings](https://www.iiba.org/business-analysis-blogs/insight-enables-confidence-the-2026-global-state-of-business-analysis-report/) | AI is used for drafting, documentation, and ideation; interpersonal judgment remains relevant. | Public summary of a practitioner survey; the member report was not reviewed. Self-reported adoption and sentiment do not establish causal productivity gains. |
| [IREB: AI4RE syllabus and study guide, v1.0.0](https://hub.ireb.org/media/pages/resources/ai4re-syllabus-and-study-guide/2621f8b1fd-1787039936/ai4re_study-guide_en_v1.0.0.pdf), 2026-03-01 | Requirements synthesized with AI need source context, human validation, and checks for fabricated, incomplete, or misleading interpretations. | Professional education guidance, not a controlled study proving this skill's effectiveness. |
| [Rani, Svensson, Feldt: AI for Requirements Engineering: Industry adoption and Practitioner perspectives, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01324) | Practitioner experiences motivate collaboration and attention to adoption barriers. | A small, self-selected cross-sectional sample spanning software roles; not representative of all BAs and not causal outcome evidence. |
| [Anthropic: Demystifying evals for AI agents, 2026-01-09](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents) | Evaluate task outcomes and variability, with appropriate automated and human assessment. | Vendor engineering guidance; testing methods must fit the product and accepted business rules. |

Apply the AI-agent product requirements only when relevant. They extend ordinary rules, permissions, and recovery analysis to delegated actions; they are not a claim that all BA work became agent design in 2025/2026.

Refresh when evidence changes a practical decision, a relevant source is superseded, or evaluations expose a failure. Check dates and versions; retain limitations alongside conclusions. No calendar-based research or external data collection is implied.
